# Character Part Detail Prompts

Use these prompts if extra part sheets are needed before modeling. They are written to produce orthographic, modeler-friendly detail images rather than cinematic concept art.

## Global Prompt Rules

- Neutral gray studio background.
- Orthographic product design sheet.
- No dramatic perspective.
- Include front, side, back, and underside/cutaway where relevant.
- Label major parts with small unobtrusive text.
- Keep design language consistent with worn off-white armor, red repair panels, dark gunmetal frame, amber barrier emitters, and cyan sensors.
- Avoid organic creature forms; this is industrial resistance mech hardware.

## Negative Prompt

```text
fantasy armor, medieval, cloth cape, smooth toy plastic, clean sports car finish, anime face, human skin, organic muscles, magic runes, unreadable clutter, extreme perspective, cinematic motion blur, smoke covering details
```
